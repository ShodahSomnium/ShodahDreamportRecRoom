## The 2025 Paintball lobby
Source: "RecRoom's New Paintball Lobby!", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c-lS_VPTyjc (8:37, about Oct 2025). The lobby
replaced the old forest-clearing deck. What the first build made (a plank deck, team pads and a console) is NOT this.

**Setting.** An OUTDOOR compound on grass, with an asphalt strip or path across the front. Mountains in the distance;
pines and autumn trees (orange and red leaves) around it; a sunny blue sky with white clouds. Bright, cartoony, clean.

**Practice Arena (the 1v1 spot):**
- A large chain-link-fenced enclosure (wire mesh on dark wooden posts).
- The entrance is a wooden gateway arch: two dark wood posts and a crossbeam, topped by an orange sign "PRACTICE ARENA"
  in cream lettering.
- Next to the gate, a big screen board shows "READY?" before a match and a "VS" card with the two players' names.
- Inside: orange shipping containers with the white Rec Room bucket logo, black tyre stacks, wooden pallet barricades,
  black barrels on the containers, a birch tree, grass floor.

**Around the plaza:**
- **Picnic tables** in orange-brown wood on the grass.
- **Leaderboard:** a big white-and-yellow board on the front of a small grey building with a blue trim roof. It reads
  "LEADERS" and "PAINTBALL WINS" and has columns of names, with "WINS" and "GLOBAL" tabs.
- **The main building:** red-orange walls, a white arched door frame round a wooden door, a curved sign over the door
  with the Rec Room hand/bucket logo ("PAINTBALL"), and teal doors and lockers beside it.
- **Tyre stacks** with a small grey pedestal carrying an orange button.

**The locker / shop building, inside:**
- Grey-brown walls and dark grey floor, with clerestory windows high up.
- Red and teal lockers covered in stickers, and stacked cardboard boxes.
- A "CHOOSE YOUR SKINS" sign with an arrow.
- A lounge corner: purple couches and armchairs; a red rug with an orange border and the Rec Room bucket logo; a
  cardboard box as a table; paintball posters on the walls and a "KILLER CLOWN ESCAPED" newspaper page.
- **The skins wall:** cork-board panels with weapon-skin displays and purple price tags ("Customize your Gear"; the
  Paintball Gear store is a watch-style panel with Supplies / Gear Skins / Pistols / Burst Rifles / Shotguns / Snipers /
  Grenade Launchers / Paint Throwers / Shields).
- **The test wall:** a grey wall headed "Try 'em out!", with each weapon type hung in white or grey and the bucket logo
  in the middle.

**Shooting gallery:**
- A wooden booth with a waist-high counter, opening onto a yard fenced with wooden planks.
- In the yard: round bullseye targets on posts and on cable spools, and tyre stacks.
- A yellow "START" button on the counter, and a small score sign ("NOW" / "BEST").

**A boombox radio.** Rec Room's radio panel: Prev / Play / Next, loop and shuffle, "Now playing: ... River".
