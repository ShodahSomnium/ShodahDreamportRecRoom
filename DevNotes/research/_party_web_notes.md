3D Charades: 2-8 players, released 2016-07-06; Maker/Charades Pen; modes Co-op Classic + Team Lightning; drawing time
 1 min (co-op) / 45 s (Team Lightning) since 2020-09; venue = The Ink Space (underground cafe, stage, couches), base room.
 Screen mode draw mode ("finger painting"), shapes cube/sphere/cylinder/pyramid..., tube size by trigger/speed.
 Guessers cannot grab drawing when not their turn.
Stunt Runner: 1-4 players, 2019-09-05, six courses (Anticipation, Daring, Patience, Caution, Trust, Perseverance),
 3 min max per course, total time -> rank S+..C (S+ < 4:45 total). climbing rope grids, wall run (jump off ~20 deg),
 slide (crouch while sprinting), KO Cola boost cans (stackable duration), moving platforms, bounce pads, red caution
 blocks KO -> last checkpoint. sprint does nothing. Custom: StuntRunner circuit board + game rules chip, respawn points,
 checkpoints, trigger volumes->respawn, StuntRunner Button (finish), leaderboard.
Rec Rally: 6 players, 2021-09-29, one off-road course, 3 laps (~2-3 min each), buggies (enter by grabbing door/trunk),
 nitro gas cans x3 -> boost, drift lever; PC: WASD steer, LShift drift, Space boost, Esc exit. Podium top 3.
 Course: dirt road, 2 turns, hill, red barn (hay, cutout chickens/horse), ramp, steep hill, big downhill turn w/ boosts,
 rocky inclines, tunnel, bridge, narrow drift point (red/white arrows), hill, railway (right turn) with 2 exits: crate
 cave or big jump; after jump straight+curve to finish or follow river to hidden jump to finish.
MITM: 4v1 (5 players), 2023-08-17, Rec County Fairground "Merry Midway". survivors repair 3 generators (sit on seat,
 skill-check dial: green=ok 25xp, light green=crit 50xp, fail -10% & alerts Bonky), stamina 100 (sprint 1, jump 5,
 slide 5, climb 40), injured->heal by teammate hold ~10 s, 2nd hit -> cage 1:30, rescue by grabbing cage door; after 3
 gens: north+south exit gates with lever; escape +550 XP. Bonky: squeaky hammer, ~3 s reflate after swing, fear aura
 20 m, abilities (jump button): Clown Radar (25 s, 75 s cd, lvl2), Silly Sneak (10 s, 45 s cd, lvl12), Bonky Bounce
 (75 s cd, lvl36). 10 min: Bonky speeds up; 15 min: midnight -> all survivors revealed. Photo booth spawns. Bathroom
 stalls hide; door hit stuns. Levels to 100, perks, tickets.
My Little Monsters: 1-8 players, 2024-06-13, roleplay in Cryptid Creek; monsters Sasquatch/Mothman/Chupacabra,
 colours blue/green/orange/pink/red(+black). Sanctuary (decorate w/ General Store crafted items). Stages Hatchling
 300, Toddler 400, Child 600, Teen 800, Adult 1000, Elder. Interactions Pet/Sit/Feed(fruit tree)/Play(toy ball)/Sleep/
 Work out/Music/Cuddle (20 xp, 100 if wanted). The Evergrown: gather with axe/pickaxe (stone/metal/hardened/everwood),
 monster light stars 3..10 (1 min each), 7 s without light -> removed; Sunstone refills. NPCs Clarence (HQ missions),
 Penny (eggs), Wilson (general store), Benedict (clothes), Iris (prismatic essence), DJ. Made in Rooms 2.0.
Punch Up: never-released 2016 boxing Original ("boxing" in early builds): orange gym, 2 white balls each side;
